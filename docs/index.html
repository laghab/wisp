What you get

Everything Gboard does, minus the watching.

A Rust core handles touch, gestures, the word list and glide matching, so keys respond in a couple of milliseconds.

〰️

Glide typing

Swipe across the letters to type a word. It learns your words and how you swipe, and a short daily practice mode sharpens it.

🎙️

Voice on the device

Words appear as you speak. The speech model loads only while you dictate and frees itself after a few idle minutes.

✨

AI rewrite, offline

Polish, shorten, make it friendly or formal. A small local model rewrites your text, and nothing is replaced until you say so.

🎨

11 real skins

Not colour swaps: shadows, fonts, glass blur, clipped corners, your own photo. Material You follows your wallpaper.

↔️

Edit mode, in place

Move the keyboard up, leave a gap below, resize it like cropping a picture, or go one-handed, split or floating. No trip to settings.

📋

Clipboard and emoji

History that skips passwords and sensitive copies, pins, and clears itself in an hour. Emoji, kaomoji and symbols with search.

Gestures

Your thumbs already know these.

Every gesture is handled in the Rust core, so they feel instant.

Glide

Swipe over letters to type a whole word.

Delete words

Hold backspace and slide left to remove whole words.

Move the cursor

Slide a finger along the space bar.

Hold for more

Long-press a key for accents and symbols.

Themes

Eleven skins. Tap one.

Each theme is a small JSON file: fonts, shadows, borders, glow, even clipped corners. Add your own.

Privacy

Private by construction, not by promise.

The keyboard cannot reach the internet, so there is nothing to opt out of.

  • No INTERNET permission in the keyboard app. The optional model downloader is a separate module, off by default.
  • Everything Wisp learns sits in a file on your phone that you can see, back up or erase.
  • Private mode pauses learning with one tap.
  • Pinned libraries: native engines are checksummed in the repo, and builds are reproducible.
