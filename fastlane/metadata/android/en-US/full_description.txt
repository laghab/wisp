<b>Talk to your keyboard. It never phones home.</b>

Wisp is a keyboard where voice typing runs on your phone. Tap the microphone and your words appear as you speak, using a speech model that stays on the device. No account, no cloud, no recordings sent anywhere. Pick the model that suits your phone (English-only models that are small and fast, Parakeet V3 for 25 European languages, Whisper for 99 languages including Hindi), and test its speed on your own phone.

<b>Voice typing</b>
• Tap once to talk hands-free, or hold the mic to talk while you hold it
• Undo dictation removes exactly what was inserted
• The model loads only while you dictate and is freed after a few idle minutes

<b>Typing that learns you</b>
• Glide typing with no download and no Google library. It learns your words and how you swipe, with a practice mode
• Autocorrect, next-word suggestions, English and Hinglish (Hindi in Latin letters)
• Hold backspace and slide to delete whole words, slide on the space bar to move the cursor

<b>A writing assistant on the phone</b>
Select text and tap the sparkle: Polish, Friendly, Formal, Shorter, Bullets or Gen Z, written by a small AI model that runs on your phone. Nothing changes until you accept the result.

<b>Yours to shape</b>
• 30 themes, each with a light and a dark look, favourites that shuffle daily or weekly
• Move, resize, raise, split, float or one-hand the keyboard from the keyboard itself
• Clipboard history that skips passwords, emoji with search, number row, six letter layouts
• Key sounds and haptics with strength sliders

<b>Private by design</b>
• Typing, swiping, learning and dictation never use the network
• The only network feature is an optional downloader for speech and rewrite models, off until you press a button
• Everything Wisp learns is a file on your phone that you can see, export or erase
• Open source, GPL-3.0

Speech and rewrite models are not part of the app. Download one from inside Wisp, or import a file.
